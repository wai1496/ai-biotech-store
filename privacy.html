Privacy Policy

AI BioTech respects the privacy of visitors, account holders and customers. This policy explains the categories of information the website may process and how that information is used to operate the store, fulfil orders and protect the service.

Information we process

Depending on how you use the service, information may include account and contact details, delivery address and telephone number, order and transaction records, product selections, customer-service communications, device/browser information and security or audit records.

How information is used

Information is used to provide accounts and store functions, process and administer orders, arrange delivery, provide invoices and order updates, prevent abuse, troubleshoot the service, maintain records and comply with applicable obligations.

Payments

Payment services may be provided by external payment processors. Sensitive payment credentials should be handled by the relevant payment provider rather than stored directly in the public storefront.

Shipping and logistics

Information necessary to fulfil an order—such as recipient name, telephone number, delivery address, parcel information and order/shipment references—may be shared with logistics platforms and selected courier providers. AI BioTech plans to use EasyParcel as a fulfilment layer, with courier services such as SPX Xpress and DHL eCommerce where available.

Cookies and local storage

The website may use cookies or similar browser storage for essential sessions, preferences, cart functions, security and, where enabled with appropriate consent, analytics or promotional features.

Security and access

Administrative and integration credentials are intended to remain server-side. Access controls, authentication, audit records and database policies are used to reduce unauthorized access. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Retention

Information is retained for as long as reasonably required for store operations, fulfilment, support, security, record keeping and applicable legal or accounting requirements, after which it may be deleted or anonymized where appropriate.

Your choices

You may contact AI BioTech regarding access, correction or other privacy questions concerning information associated with your account, subject to applicable requirements and legitimate record-retention needs.

Third-party services

Third-party services used by the website may process information under their own privacy terms. These may include hosting, database, payment, email, analytics and logistics providers.

Policy changes

This policy may be updated as the website and its integrations develop. The current version will be published on this page.

Contact

Privacy enquiries can be submitted through the AI BioTech website contact channel.
